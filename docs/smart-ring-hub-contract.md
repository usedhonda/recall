# Direct Ring Health: Hub Reader Contract

This extends the existing Recall health routes, not provisioning, domains or
permissions. Sleep-protocol work is separate and is not part of this contract.

## Raw Observations

`recall.health-samples.v1` uses the existing `metadata-raw` lane. Its source
payload has `items` and `deleted`. Ring items use `type: smart_ring.hrv_history`.
The item UUID identifies the original ring observation across retries. Source
version, pseudonymous device ID, firmware, explicit clock zone, original device
clock and all bytes are retained in the `ring` object. `start`/`end` are the
interpreted measurement time; the envelope capture time is not that measurement.

The `ring` fields are `deviceID`, `firmware`, `clockTimeZone`, `deviceTime`,
`hrvRaw`, `heartRateBPM`, `stressRaw`, and `rawBytes`. A reader must preserve
unknown fields instead of interpreting them as additional physiological values.

## Selected Health View

`recall.health-snapshot.v1` carries the combined view in `source_payload.records`:

| metricId | unit | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `HKQuantityTypeIdentifierHeartRate` | `count/min` | Positive ring pulse, selected according to the source-time policy |
| `smart_ring.hrv_raw` | `raw` | SDK-labelled HRV byte; algorithm and unit unknown |
| `smart_ring.stress_raw` | `raw` | SDK-labelled stress byte; scale and direction unknown |

All direct records have `aggregation: latest`, their actual `measuredAt`,
`source: SmartRingKit`, `sourceObservationID` and hashed `sourceDeviceID`.
Multiple metrics can refer to one original observation; use metric ID plus source
observation ID when relating records, not observation ID alone.

`sourceRecords` is the original candidate set, not another set to aggregate with
`records`. Keep it for provenance and alternate-source inspection. Use `records`
for the selected health view; do not add HealthKit and ring pulse together.
Daily heart-rate aggregates, sleep and unsupported measurements still come from
HealthKit. They do not become ring-derived merely because the same snapshot has
ring records. See [selection policy](smart-ring.md#unified-health-snapshot).

## Interpretation And Time

- Never convert raw HRV to SDNN or assign milliseconds.
- Never label raw stress as a calibrated percent, clinical threshold or diagnosis.
- Retain raw zeros without assigning valid/missing meaning.
- Use measurement time for freshness and ordering, not delivery/collection time.
- Do not replace a newer measurement with a later-delivered older snapshot.
- Keep the two routes' duplicate provenance; they are not extra measurements to sum.

## Acceptance Layers

The synthetic wire example is generated by the actual Recall encoders in
`SmartRingTests.testUnifiedHealthPrefersRingPulseWithoutLosingHealthKitOrConvertingRawHRV`.
Its attachment is explicitly synthetic and must not be posted to production.
It contains both source payload shapes, not credentials or a complete ingest
request. Receiver fixture acceptance, production deployment, real BLE capture,
phone committed receipt, scoped read and consumer interpretation are separate
facts. A green synthetic test proves no real ring delivery.

The generated [wire example](smart-ring-hub.fixture.json) passed an isolated Hub
Store round trip and the existing scoped stdio reader, including complete
pagination (raw: 4 pages; snapshot: 14 pages at a test limit of 200). Reassembled
metadata matched exactly and an unrelated synthetic reader without health scope
was denied. No synthetic data was posted to production. This confirms the wire,
storage and scoped-read shape, not the still-separate health dashboard projection
or real ring receipt.

Hub implementation `bdbf8ac` subsequently added `ring_readings`, a separate latest
projection for these two metrics ordered by `measuredAt`, and the corresponding
raw-value health tiles/preview. This projection is outside the daily/monthly
rollup inputs, so it does not extend raw retention. Four affected receiver checks
passed, including old-arrival ordering, repeated observations, zero preservation
and excluding source candidates/rollups. Parent review inspected the integrated
diff and the actual health-view synthetic rendering, not another suite run.
The existing dashboard process was reloaded onto the committed implementation;
unauthenticated viewer access remained denied. Real ring capture/receipt/display
is still unverified while direct capture is OFF. No synthetic production upload,
new API or reader permission was introduced, and neither repository was pushed.

The ring remains OFF until device selection and explicit clock-zone setup are
complete. The history-retention floor applies to both raw and selected data.
